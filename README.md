### ALGO-000208: Decidability of 4-sphere recognition
- **Subfield:** Computational number theory, lattices, and topology
- **Open:** Given a triangulated closed four-manifold, decide whether it is homeomorphic to \(S^4\), or prove that no algorithm exists.
- **Status:** Three-sphere recognition is decidable; sphere recognition in sufficiently high dimensions is undecidable. Dimension four remains exceptional and unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines a fundamental computability boundary in low-dimensional topology.
- **Key works:** Novikov’s sphere-recognition undecidability work; Poonen, *Undecidable Problems: A Sampler*; recent computational-topology discussions.
- **Related:** Four-manifold topology—recognition and classification.
